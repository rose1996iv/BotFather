# GEU Program Update Notes

As of 2026-05-11, the official GEU website and GEU Aerospace brochure show that Aerospace is available at Graphic Era University.

## Verified Aerospace availability

- GEU official admissions overview lists `B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering`.
- GEU B.Tech admissions page includes `Aerospace Engineering` in the available B.Tech departments.
- GEU Aerospace brochure states these offerings:
  - `B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering`
  - `B.Tech in Aerospace Engineering (Lateral Entry)`
  - `Ph.D in Aerospace Engineering`
  - Specializations: `Space Technology`, `Drone Technology`

## Aerospace subjects and curriculum themes

When a student asks what Aerospace teaches, prefer these curriculum/module topics from the GEU Aerospace brochure:

- Aerodynamics
- Supersonic and Hypersonic Aerodynamics
- Helicopter Aerodynamics and Wind Tunnel Techniques
- Aerospace Propulsion
- Rocket Propulsion
- Aircraft Structures
- Flight Mechanics
- Aircraft Stability and Control
- Space Dynamics
- Orbital Mechanics
- Drone Technology
- Navigation, Guidance and Control
- Composite Materials
- Finite Element Analysis
- Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD)
- Aircraft Design
